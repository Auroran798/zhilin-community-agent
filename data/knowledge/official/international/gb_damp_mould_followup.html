Report summary

Many landlords have engaged positively with the Spotlight report on damp and mould, since its publication in 2021. The report has been downloaded over 5,000 times.

Following the inquest into Awaab Ishak, the Ombudsman wrote an open letter to all landlords. It reinforced the recommendations made in the Spotlight report on damp and mould.

We know that many landlords have been revising their approach to damp and mould. This update aims to provide further insights to landlords as they continue their work in this area. It also supports governing bodies when reviewing self-assessments against our 26 recommendations.

What is in the follow up report?

This report focuses on:

  • damp and mould data from our casework in 2021-22
  • summarising the outcome of follow up work with a number of landlords to understand how they are implementing the recommendations, highlighting areas of best practice as well as identifying areas of continued concern
  • important lessons from our recent casework on severe damp and mould failures
  • providing governing bodies with a set of 10 key factors that will influence the success of any action plan to support their scrutiny role
